Privacy Policy

We Grow With Islam Shorts — last updated October 13, 2026.

What data this app accesses

Through Google OAuth, this app accesses the channel owner's own YouTube data only:

  • YouTube uploads (youtube.upload) — to publish Shorts videos to the owner's channel
  • YouTube channel & video metadata (youtube.readonly, youtube) — to verify publish status, manage playlists, and read video details
  • YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly) — to read view and subscriber statistics

How the data is used

Data is used solely to automate publishing and monitor the performance of the owner's own YouTube channel. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data storage

OAuth tokens are stored securely on the owner's private server and never exposed publicly. Videos and metadata processed by the app belong to the channel owner.

Data deletion

The owner may revoke access at any time via Google Account permissions, which immediately invalidates all tokens.
